ARABIC SUBTITLE OPTIMIZATION

Automatically fix inverted punctuation, brackets, and quotes in Arabic subtitles to prevent misplaced periods (e.g. .مرحبا -→- مرحبا.).

Removes Harakat while keeping Shadda, Tanween, and feminine Kasra (e.g. أنتِ، لكِ، علّمتِ).

Converts Latin commas in Arabic text to Arabic commas (e.g. نعم , لا → نعم، لا).

Converts Western digits to Eastern Arabic numerals in Arabic dialogue (e.g. قبل 3 أيام→قبل ٣ أيام).

Dialogue & Clean-up

Filter out subtitles with sound effects and audio descriptions. (Hides entire HI subtitle tracks from the list)

Clean sound effects, audio cues, and speaker tags (e.g. [LAUGHS], (SIGHS), or JOHN:) while keeping dialogue intact. (Keeps the subtitle track, but removes the HI text inside it)

Clean promotional links, website domains, and social media handles from subtitles automatically.

Preserve translator names while removing spam, ads, and website links.

Text Formatting & Timing

Fixes unclosed <i>/<b> tags and repairs malformed wrappers.

Removes ASS/inline custom font colors to enforce a clean, player-native uniform text color.

Enhances playback stability on Android TV (ExoPlayer) by converting ASS/SSA to SRT while retaining primary text colors and fixing Arabic RTL alignment.

Collapses multi-spaces and fixes word , next → word, next.

Normalizes loose dashes -- → ... and cleans stray <br>.

Clamps micro-overlaps (<500ms) between consecutive cues to stop player flickering.
