# Boundary production evidence gate

EDGE_DISCONTINUITY remains shadow-only. Capture, replay, or import code creates
no production verification authority. Normal delivery, hash invariants,
language independence, ALASS order, reference selection, original provider cache,
Large Offset's transformed-but-unverified exception and all thresholds remain
unchanged.

Promotion requires ALL of:

1. At least two independent replayable REAL EDGE_DISCONTINUITY positives.
2. At least two independent replayable REAL meaningful rejection cases.
3. Zero observed false positives and unnecessary escalation of globally solvable
   cases, with independently assigned expected outcomes.
4. No weakened safety gates and zero introduced safety regressions.
5. Known S08E04, Whiplash and derived negative regressions pass.
6. Before actual serving authorization, final response must be independently
   verified or cleaner timing preservation proven. Raw success alone is not
   proof of final served synchronization. Production promotion also needs the
   next explicitly authorized milestone; the numerical gate never deploys code.

Replayable REAL requires independent live origin, SHA-bound artifacts, identical
verifier semantics, and deterministic agreement with the independent expectation.
Capture V2 requires original-to-decoder-to-shadow-input proof, raw/reference/final
hashes, parser implementation fingerprint and regenerated cue manifests.
Canonical cue flags are projections, not original subtitle artifacts.

The already accepted historical S08E04 timing/eligibility manifest remains the
single canonical regression positive. Its existing origin hashes and pinned
manifest hash are recorded in tests/replay/evidence_gate.json. This narrowly
preserves the accepted historical baseline; it does not grandfather other V1
captures or recreate lost dialogue. Repeated S08E04 runs count once.

Whiplash is a replayable REAL global affine control. It counts as a normal REAL
negative/control, NOT as a meaningful incompatible-cut/structural hard negative.
A future meaningful negative needs independent labeling of a relevant unsafe
case, not merely a detector-produced refusal or lack of references.

S08E02 and S08E03 are SUPPORTING_REAL_LIVE_NEGATIVE: proven live origin/hashes and
reproduced cue_population_changed. Their pristine import/content contract fails;
they do not satisfy numerical replayable minima. Historical files stay untouched.
Three incomplete S08E06 captures and the unproven desktop triplet remain UNKNOWN.
S08E01 had repeated zero-reference attempts; do not repeat arbitrary variants.

DERIVED and SYNTHETIC remain separate controls. UNKNOWN never becomes REAL through
release-name similarity. Independence uses target/reference artifact identity;
repeated raw output variants for the same pair cannot inflate numerical minima.
A repeated supporting capture of a replayable pair also counts once. The numerical
REAL gate additionally counts at most one case per proven media/episode identity;
changing references or raw output for S08E04 cannot manufacture a second positive.

`tools/boundary_evidence_gate.py` computes a deduplicated inventory from the corpus
and optional local captures. Only the two independently confirmed historical
negative identities are recognized as supporting REAL. All other incomplete or
unproven sets remain UNKNOWN. No case is imported by this inventory tool. It makes
no network, cache, or ALASS calls. Safety regression count must be explicitly
supplied from a completed validation pass; missing attestation cannot pass.

Current numerical status: replayable edge positives 1; replayable meaningful
negatives 0. Supporting live negatives 2 are outside these minima. Missing:
one additional independent replayable REAL edge positive and two independently
labeled replayable REAL meaningful negatives. New faithful V2 recaptures may
satisfy negative requirements only after explicit import/replay validation.
All future live observations remain unadmitted until checked. No thresholds are
relaxed to obtain an example.
